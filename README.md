## What this is

One funny line, 105 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock.

> I did not eat your sock, I was just giving it a tour of the house and it decided to stay under the couch.

The robot vacuum denies eating the sock. It was only giving the sock a tour of the house, and the sock decided to stay under the couch. The joke stays in the vacuum's voice, no extra punchline bolted on.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no network, deterministic) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
